# Dataset attribution

The demo audio in samples/, demo_audio/ and examples/ is derived from VOICED Database version 1.0.0, available at https://physionet.org/content/voiced/1.0.0/ , DOI https://doi.org/10.13026/C25Q2N . The dataset is distributed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/ . Retain attribution when redistributing database-derived material. The samples are central-three-second derivatives, not new clinical recordings.

Cesari U, De Pietro G, Marciano E, Niri C, Sannino G, Verde L. A new database of healthy and pathological voices. Computers & Electrical Engineering 68 (2018), 310–321. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compeleceng.2018.04.008

Reference review: Gupta R et al. Voice disorders classification using machine learning: a scoping review. Frontiers in Digital Health 8 (2026), 1800132. https://doi.org/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1800132
